Our mission

We promote the mental wellness of individuals through initiatives that provide communal support and education, so each of us can embark on our own healing journey.

Our approach: matching grants and hands-on support

We partner with organizations that have proven success in the causes we support. We don't just write checks: we serve on boards and give hands-on help with governance, financial management, fundraising, and human resource strategy.

About us

Founded in 2024, Dance with Depression is a Utah-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit that promotes mental wellness and helps people manage depressive symptoms through communal support and education.

After losing her husband to suicide in 1998, Dr. Therese Chan, a family doctor, founded Dance with Depression Hong Kong, which has touched the lives of more than 600 people over 24+ years. In 2024 she teamed up with her daughter Eva Chan to start this sister nonprofit in Utah.

Therese serves as Secretary, Eva as Chairperson, and Lorraine Chan as Treasurer. Together we approve matched donations and monitor that partners spend them wisely. We welcome a conversation about partnering with your organization.
